# Verified facts

Facts about LDraw parts, licenses, dimensions, connector poses and module standards that code or data relies on. Add rows with the `verify-ldraw-facts` skill (`.cursor/skills/verify-ldraw-facts/SKILL.md`). Never add a row from memory.

## Inputs

There is one LDraw pin, the one the bundle ships: `palette.json` `source`, which is `kulits/ldraw-parts@c70360d1c588be894997b2168c61909bc8058c4d` (`ldraw/`). That mirror is a daily, unmodified unzip of the official `complete.zip`, here library update 2026-10. LDCad connectivity comes from `palette.json` `connectivitySource`, `RolandMelkert/LDCadShadowLibrary@9b1131fb1991f8c0bfc072325e4e12f6271aba35` (CC BY-SA 4.0). It's used for verification only, and is never committed or bundled.

`pnpm --filter @brr/asset-pipeline fetch-ldraw` downloads, with the one fetcher (`src/fetch-ldraw.ts`):

- the bundle closure (palette parts plus every part the `models` reference) into `.cache/ldraw`, and its LDCad shadow files into `.cache/ldcad-shadow-bundle`;
- the closure of every part in `palette.json` `audit` into `.cache/ldraw-audit`;
- the matching LDCad patches into `.cache/ldcad-shadow`.

Both closures follow `SNAP_INCL` as well as subfile references: a pinned shadow file's include target joins the closure with its own LDraw closure and their shadow files, so the bundle and the audit read every shadow file the full library would.

Every file is pinned by SHA-256 in `ldraw.lock.json`: `files` for the bundle and `audit` for the rest. None of them is committed; `restore-ldraw` downloads exactly the pinned files (checking each hash) without re-resolving anything. A download retries a 408, a 429, a 5xx or a transient network error (a reset, refused or timed-out connection, a failed DNS lookup, or a body cut off mid-read) for up to six attempts, about 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s apart with ±25 % jitter, or longer when `Retry-After` asks for more (at most 30 s), and logs each retry with the URL and the failure. Any other answer ends it at once: a 404 means the pinned file is missing upstream and fails the restore, and a file whose hash doesn't match the pin fails it too, never retried and never written. `facts` re-checks those hashes and writes `docs/verified-facts/license-audit.json`; `facts --check` reports drift instead. `library.ldraw.org`, Rebrickable and the MILS site are blocked from the verification environment; the sources below were fetched elsewhere.

## Geometry

All geometry is measured at the bundle's pin, from the `audit` closure that `fetch-ldraw` pins, by `tools/asset-pipeline/src/verify/geometry.ts` (run through `cli.ts facts`). The full output is in `docs/verified-facts/geometry.json`. Values are **as modelled in LDraw**, in the LDraw part frame (−Y up, 1 LDU = 0.4 mm), before any y-up conversion. Rail measurements (`rails[]`) are exact: each vertex is composed from the LDraw decimal literals with exact decimal arithmetic, so rail heads, widths and gauges are written as canonical decimal strings (53401's head is `46.9` to `53.1`, 6.2 LDU wide) and read back with `@brr/math` `parseExactDecimal`. 53401's ±46.9/±53.1 aren't sixteenths, so they're never identified to the 1/16 grid.

Track ends are the origins of the end-sleeper sub-files listed in `tools/asset-pipeline/src/track/track-end-markers.json`, with the outward direction taken from each placement's matrix. The report checks every declared outward direction against the part's bounds (all agree), and the LDCad `traintrk` snaps against the marker origins (they coincide).

Relative poses are measured from the end with the least x, rotated so that its inward direction is +x. They're written in studs as ℤ[ζ₁₆] coefficients (c₀…c₇) of Σ cₖζᵏ, with x → Re and z → Im. A pose is **certified** under the project rule (decided by the project, delegated by the owner, 2026-11-16): certified iff residual ≤ 0.0005 LDU per coordinate (read per coordinate, approved 2026-11-18), half the last decimal of 3-decimal LDraw data. The residual is measured against a candidate, which must be the only coefficient vector within one unit of the file's last printed decimal in the search box |c₀|, |c₄| ≤ 64 and |cₖ ± c₈₋ₖ| ≤ 64 (k = 1…3). With no candidate, or more than one, there is nothing to certify. `geometry.json` records coefficients only for certified ends; for the others it records the search result and `null`.

The same command writes two LDraw scenes. `docs/verified-facts/scenes/switch-then-reversed-curve.ldr` places its parts from the measured track-end poses, with placement rotations snapped to the 11.25° grid. `docs/verified-facts/scenes/road-baseplates.ldr` abuts the plates edge to edge using their measured body extents (`baseplates[].zExtent`), `scenes/road-plates.ldr` does the same for the recessed-stud road plates 69958 and 71772, and `scenes/baseplate-row.ldr` for a 3811 beside a 44336. `scenes/r40-circle-9v.ldr` and `scenes/oval-passing-siding-9v.ldr` place every part from its exact ℤ[ζ₁₆] frame, composed with `@brr/math`; floats appear only in the written matrices. All four are listed under `palette.json` `models`, so the bundle build bakes them like any model, and the app renders them at `/?model=<scene name>`. The screenshots in `docs/verified-facts/evidence/` were taken there with the WebGL2 backend. Parts use colour 16 (LDConfig main colour), because no produced colour is claimed.

## Track catalog

`cli.ts facts` builds the track catalog from every palette part with role `track` (`tools/asset-pipeline/src/track/track-catalog.ts`) and writes it, with its checks, to `docs/verified-facts/track-catalog.json`. `cli.ts bundle` copies the pieces into each bundle manifest as `trackCatalog`, and `@brr/catalog` parses them (`parseTrackCatalog`). The catalog is in the bundle's +Y-up frame: z = −z_LDraw, converted once in the pipeline. Connector 0 is the reference (origin, outward heading 8), and every heading is outward.

- **Connector poses** come from the end-sleeper markers. They're certified by the §5.3 field test or, failing that, derived and marked `unverified`. A missing end is a build error.
- **Routes** pair ends whose outward headings differ by 7, 8 or 9 steps; the piece kind follows from that structure.
- **Route paths** are fitted to the rail heads. The catalog takes the flat polygons at the rail-top level found at the reference end. It then picks the path family (straight; arc; arc then straight; straight then arc; two reversed equal-radius arcs) that reproduces the exact end poses and keeps the most of both rails, at ± the measured half rail spacing, on rail-head polygons, sampled every 2 LDU.
- **Independent check:** at 20 LDU inside every end, a lateral scan of the rail heads finds two rails, and the catalog records the end's offset from their midpoint.
